## Consent

By providing your mobile number through this website or by text, you agree that Birmingham Foundation Repair and the local contractor handling your request may contact you by text message about your inquiry, your inspection and the work you asked about.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service. You can ask us to call or email instead.

## What We Send

Messages relate directly to your inquiry: confirming an appointment, letting you know a crew is on the way, following up on a quote, or asking a question about the work.

We do not send marketing campaigns or promotional blasts to numbers collected through this site.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your inquiry. In most cases it is a small number of messages relating to a single job.

## Charges

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan and carrier. We do not charge for text messages; your carrier may.

## Opting Out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that.

Opting out of text messages does not prevent us from contacting you by phone or email about an active inquiry, unless you ask us not to.

## Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at info@bhamfoundationrepair.com or (205) 555-0100.

## Carrier Liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Your mobile number is handled in line with our privacy policy. It is not sold, and it is not passed to marketing lists.

## Changes

If these terms change, the updated version will be posted on this page.

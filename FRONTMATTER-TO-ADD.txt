WHY THESE WERE RE-CROPPED AND RE-ENCODED
===================================================================
They came out of ChatGPT at 1448x1086 (4:3) as PNGs of 2 to 3.2 MB
each. Two problems with using those as-is.

The page crops heroes to 2:1. Letting CSS do that to a 4:3 image cuts
a third of the height off, and it cuts from wherever the CSS decides —
which on the stair-step crack would have taken the top of the crack,
the part that does all the work. Each of these was cropped by hand
instead, biased per image toward whatever the photo is actually about.

PNG is the wrong format for photographs. Sixteen megabytes of hero
images would have been the slowest thing on the site by a wide margin,
and page speed is both a ranking input and, more importantly, the
thing that decides whether someone on a phone in a driveway waits for
your page to load. As JPEGs at quality 82 they total 1.46 MB.

If you regenerate any of these, ask for 2:1 and convert to JPEG before
they go in public/images/. Never commit a multi-megabyte PNG.
